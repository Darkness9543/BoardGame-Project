Coin Duel Action Bar Options

Three arrangements of the command area, all showing the same moment: your Berserker 1 (8 Power, 3 Toughness) is selected with Move previewed, 19 of 20 AP left, 8 units still able to act. The current bar is shown first for comparison. Icons are the game's own.

Reference

The current bar

One row of same-weight pills with inline cost text; abilities and basic actions look alike; it scrolls sideways when it runs out of room; disabled reasons live only in tooltips.

Option A

Command dock

The bar becomes a dock of large tiles. Icon on top, name below, AP cost in the corner, hotkey in the other corner. Basic actions and abilities are separate groups; abilities carry a cooldown ring. A disabled tile stays visible and says why in one line, so nothing needs a tooltip. The active action is framed in gold. Tiles wrap to a second row before anything scrolls.

For

  • Each action is a target you can read from the board: icon, name, cost and key at a glance.
  • Disabled actions explain themselves in place.
  • Basic actions, abilities and turn controls are visually separate groups.
  • Keeps everything in one place, under the board, where the eye already goes for End turn.

Against

  • Takes about 20 px more height than the current bar.
  • Units with many abilities (Artificer: 3, Royal wizard: 2) need the second row or a narrower tile.

Option B

Side command panel

Actions move into the left panel, under the unit card, as a vertical list with a one-line description each. The bottom bar keeps only turn controls. Nothing ever scrolls or wraps, and the full ability text sits right next to its button.

For

  • Room for a description on every action, which helps new players most.
  • Never wraps or scrolls, whatever the unit.
  • Abilities get their own labelled group with cooldown state.

Against

  • The eye travels from the board to the side and back for every action.
  • Costs about 120 px of the left panel; the unit card and forecast panel get less room.
  • Weaker sense of a single "command row" during fast play.

Option C

Radial menu on the board

Selecting a unit opens a ring of icon buttons around its coin; hotkey letters sit on the ring and costs appear above each node. The sidebar shows the description of whatever is hovered. The bottom bar keeps turn controls only.

For

  • Fastest with a mouse: the choice is one short move from the unit.
  • Spatial: the action sits on the piece it affects.
  • Frees the bottom bar entirely.

Against

  • Covers the squares around the unit, exactly where the targets are, so it fights move and attack previews.
  • Icons alone, no names: harder to learn, and hostile to colour-blind and touch players.
  • Needs a second surface for descriptions and disabled reasons anyway.
Recommended

Option A, the command dock, plus Option B's descriptions in the side panel

The dock keeps commands where the eye already goes, gives every action a readable target with its cost, key and reason, and separates abilities from basic actions. The left panel keeps the unit card and, under it, shows the full text of whatever action is hovered or armed (today's hover hint line, promoted to a proper card), so the dock never needs tooltips. Option C stays a good idea for a later touch-first mode, where it can replace the dock on phones.

If you prefer B or C, say so and I'll build that instead; the engine side is identical for all three.
